About Me

My name is Yousif Findakly and I am a Computer Science student at the University of North Florida in Jacksonville.

I started programming when I was 14. I began by building games on Roblox using Luau language, and I quickly realized that I enjoyed the process of bringing ideas to life through code. I spent countless hours learning and doing small projects until I felt confident to start releasing games. Since I have built a number of games that been played by 30,000,000 million .

Last year I launched MCATPrepLab, a company offering one-on-one MCAT tutoring with top scoring tutros. Students get a custom study plan, book sessions around their own schedule, and track their progress from an interactive dashboard. I built the backend platform myself: a React-based Next.js front end, a Postgres database behind Supabase for authentication and data, separate student, tutor, and administrator dashboards, and scheduling for live sessions.

Running it has been a different kind of lesson than building games was. Tutors and students depend on the booking system being right, so a bug is not just an inconvenience, it is a missed session someone paid for.

Hobbies and Interests

Most of my early programming experience came from building games on Roblox. The games I have worked on have passed 30 million visits combined. Scripting them in Lua taught me things a classroom could not: how to keep a server responsive under load, how to read player behavior from analytics instead of guessing, and how to ship an update without breaking the experience for everyone already online.

Working at that scale also taught me that code is only part of the job. A change that is technically correct can still be the wrong change if it confuses the people using it, and finding that out means paying attention to feedback rather than just to the error log. That lesson carried directly into running MCATPrepLab.

My hobbies include going to the gym, working on projects, and cooking.

Education

I am working toward a bachelor's degree in Information Systems at UNF, with the goal of continuing to build and run web applications professionally. The years I spent building on Roblox before college gave me a head start on the practical side, launching MCATPrepLab taught me the parts that are not code.
